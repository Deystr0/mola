# Code of conduct

We want a collaborative project where people can discuss technical decisions without harassment.
Be respectful, assume good intent, give specific feedback about work rather than people, and make
space for contributors with different experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions,
or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban an
account from project spaces. They will consider context and apply these measures proportionately.
For content hosted on GitHub, use the **Report content** control on the relevant comment or profile
to notify GitHub privately; you may also flag a concern to a repository maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Deystr0). Do not publish sensitive personal information in a
public issue. This policy applies to project spaces and to conduct representing this project.
